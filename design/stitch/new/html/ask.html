What is the policy for severe weather travel?

smart_toy

According to the revised policy_v2.pdf, travel reimbursements during severe weather events are governed by the following guidelines:

  • Rebooking Fees: Covered up to $250 if the delay exceeds 4 hours due to declared severe weather.
  • Accommodation: If stranded overnight, hotel stays are reimbursed up to the local per diem rate + 20% surge allowance.
  • Documentation: Employees must attach the airline weather delay notice alongside standard receipts.

Please consult the severity_definitions.csv to confirm if your specific weather event qualifies as 'Severe' (Level 3 or higher).
