# Executive Communications & Global All-Hands Policy
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Mara Chen, CEO (mara.chen@helixforge.example / CC-4010). Operational Lead: Priya Nair, VP Business Operations. Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Transparency & Communication Philosophy
HelixForge fosters open, candid communication across our ~420 FTEs and global offices in Austin, Dublin, and Singapore. While the written KB remains the definitive source of truth for technical policy, regular synchronous executive forums ensure alignment on corporate strategy, financial milestones, and product vision.

## 2. Meeting Cadence & Global Scheduling
- **Global All-Hands (The Forge)**: Held bi-weekly on Thursdays at 15:00 UTC (09:00 Austin CST, 15:00 Dublin GMT/IST, 23:00 Singapore SGT) during core collaboration hours (14:00–17:00 UTC).
- **Executive Town Halls & Earnings Review**: Held quarterly following board meetings, led by Mara Chen, Devon Hale, Priya Nair, and Elena Voss.
- **Async Recording Archive**: High-definition recordings and AI-transcribed executive summaries are published within 24 hours to the internal wiki for team members in APAC who prefer async viewing.

## 3. Open Q&A & Anonymous Slido Norms
- Every All-Hands includes a dedicated 20-minute open Q&A session.
- Questions are submitted and upvoted via Slido. Anonymous submissions are welcomed and answered live by executive leadership without pre-screening.
- Questions not addressed during live time are answered in written wiki follow-ups within 3 business days.

## 4. Confidentiality of Executive Briefings
Information shared during All-Hands and quarterly financial reviews is classified as Confidential (Internal Only). Sharing non-public revenue figures, customer deal sizes, or unannounced model architectures externally is a severe violation of the company Code of Conduct.
