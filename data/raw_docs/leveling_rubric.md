# Engineering & Applied Research Leveling Rubric
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Priya Nair, VP Business Operations / People Ops (priya.nair@helixforge.example). Approved by: Devon Hale (CTO) and Mara Chen (CEO). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Overview and Level Architecture
HelixForge uses a unified technical leveling ladder spanning Levels 3 through 8 (L3–L8) across Platform Engineering and Applied Research. Progression is evaluated across four core dimensions: Scope of Impact, Technical Complexity, Execution & Delivery, and Cultural & Mentorship Leadership.

All role transitions and compensation adjustments are anchored to the salary ranges defined in compensation_bands.csv.

## 2. Level Definitions & Expectations

### Level 3 (L3) – Associate Engineer
- **Scope**: Task-level execution under guided supervision within a single team pod.
- **Complexity**: Fixes bugs, implements well-scoped API endpoints, and writes unit/integration tests.
- **Collaboration**: Active participant in code reviews and daily standups; adheres to the core collaboration window (14:00–17:00 UTC).
- **On-Call**: Secondary shadowing on on-call rotation after 90 days tenure.

### Level 4 (L4) – Engineer II / Research Scientist I
- **Scope**: Feature-level ownership spanning end-to-end component lifecycles.
- **Complexity**: Designs clean modular services, manages intermediate Ray distributed workers, and conducts model benchmark sweeps.
- **Execution**: Delivers quarterly OKR commitments independently with minimal supervision.
- **On-Call**: Participates as a fully qualified primary on-call in rotations listed in oncall_escalation_matrix.csv.

### Level 5 (L5) – Senior Engineer / Senior Research Scientist
- **Scope**: Subsystem ownership spanning multiple interconnected components (e.g., Inference Gateway, Alignment Guardrails).
- **Complexity**: Authors Architectural Decision Records (ADRs), diagnoses complex distributed race conditions, and optimizes GPU memory allocations.
- **Execution**: Mentors junior team members, participates in engineering interview loops, and drives cross-functional initiatives.
- **Authorization**: Eligible to review PRs touching production infrastructure and sign off on internal change requests.

### Level 6 (L6) – Staff Engineer / Staff Research Scientist
- **Scope**: Multi-team domain leadership across entire divisions (Platform Engineering or Applied Research).
- **Complexity**: Solves architectural bottlenecks affecting platform-wide RPO/RTO metrics; designs fault-tolerant multi-region failover.
- **Strategic Impact**: Defines 12–24 month technical roadmaps in collaboration with Devon Hale (CTO).

### Level 7 (L7) – Principal Engineer / Principal Research Scientist
- **Scope**: Company-wide technical authority influencing executive decisions and strategic AI architecture.
- **Complexity**: Industry-recognized domain expert; leads high-risk zero-to-one research initiatives.

### Level 8 (L8) – Distinguished Engineer
- **Scope**: Enterprise-level technical strategy reporting directly to CTO Devon Hale or CEO Mara Chen.

## 3. Performance Review & Promotion Cycles
- **Cadence**: Formal reviews occur semi-annually in **August** and **February** (aligned with the fiscal year beginning February 1).
- **Promotion Committee**: Chaired by Priya Nair and Devon Hale. Nominations require 3 peer reviews (at or above target level) and division head sponsorship.
- **Compensation Updates**: Equity refreshes and base salary adjustments take effect on the first day of the pay cycle following committee ratification.
