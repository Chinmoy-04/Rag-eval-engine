# Interview Loop & Hiring Standards
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Priya Nair, VP Business Operations / People Ops (priya.nair@helixforge.example / CC-4010). Approved by: Devon Hale (CTO) and Mara Chen (CEO). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Hiring Principles & Evaluation Rigor
HelixForge maintains rigorous technical and cultural hiring standards to support our distributed team across Austin, Dublin, and Singapore. Every hiring loop is calibrated against the core competencies and level expectations outlined in leveling_rubric.md.

## 2. Standard 4-Stage Interview Loop Architecture
All full-time engineering and research candidates undergo four structured evaluation rounds:

1. **Initial Screen (45 mins)**: Recruiter assessment and hiring manager technical overview.
2. **Technical Deep-Dive / Live Sandbox (60 mins)**: Hands-on system design or live coding in an isolated Linux environment (Docker/Kubernetes runtime or PyTorch distributed tensor manipulation).
3. **Domain Specialist & Research Seminar (60 mins)**:
   - *Platform Engineering*: Distributed systems architecture, consensus protocols, and latency optimization.
   - *Applied Research*: Research presentation and deep-dive into model alignment, evaluation methodologies, or synthetic data pipelines.
4. **Cross-Functional & Culture Alignment (45 mins)**: Behavioral evaluation led by People Ops or a peer from Customer Success/Business Operations.

## 3. The Bar Raiser Program
- Every full-time loop includes an independent **Bar Raiser**—an L6+ Staff Engineer or Lead from an unrelated department.
- The Bar Raiser holds veto authority over candidate leveling and cultural alignment to prevent hiring compromises during periods of rapid scaling.

## 4. Scorecard Submission & Offer SLAs
- **Scorecard SLA**: Interviewers must submit independent, written scorecards in Ashby within **24 hours** of completing the interview session. Discussing candidates before scorecard submission is strictly prohibited.
- **Hiring Debrief**: Chaired by the Hiring Manager and Bar Raiser within **48 business hours** of loop completion.
- **Offer Approvals**: Compensation packages must align with compensation_bands.csv. Offers exceeding mid-band targets require approval from Priya Nair and Mara Chen per pproval_matrix.csv.
