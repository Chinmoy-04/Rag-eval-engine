# Employee Wellness & Mental Health EAP Policy
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Priya Nair, VP Business Operations / People Ops (priya.nair@helixforge.example / CC-4010). Reviewed by: Mara Chen (CEO). Effective: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Overview & Commitment
HelixForge provides comprehensive physical, mental, and emotional health support for all ~420 FTEs across Austin, Dublin, Singapore, and remote locations. Employee wellbeing is paramount in maintaining high operational rigor and sustainable engineering velocity.

## 2. Modern Health Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- **Coverage**: 100% company-paid for all employees and their recognized legal dependents.
- **Individual Therapy**: Up to **8 free sessions** with a licensed clinical therapist or psychologist per calendar year.
- **Professional Coaching**: Up to **8 free sessions** with a certified career, leadership, or mindfulness coach per year.
- **Confidentiality Guarantee**: All consultations are strictly confidential. No individual session details or attendance records are shared with HelixForge management or People Ops.

## 3. Wellness Stipend & Fitness Benefits
- **Monthly Wellness Allowance**: ** USD** (€95 EUR / S SGD) per month via monthly payroll credit.
- **Eligible Submissions**: Gym memberships, fitness equipment, yoga studio classes, meditation app subscriptions (e.g. Headspace, Calm), massage therapy, and ergonomic wellness items.

## 4. On-Call Fatigue & Decompression Hours
Engineering and support personnel participating in 24/7 on-call rotations (see oncall_escalation_matrix.csv):
- If an on-call engineer is paged for a SEV-1/SEV-2 incident between **22:00 and 06:00 local time**, they are granted **4 hours of mandatory rest decompression** the following morning.
- Normal morning meetings and standups must be excused with zero impact on performance review or leveling metrics.

## 5. Crisis Support & 24/7 Hotlines
- US Crisis Line: Dial 988 or 1-800-273-TALK.
- Ireland Crisis Helpline: 1800 247 247.
- Singapore SOS Hotline: 1-767.
- Global 24/7 Modern Health Crisis Line: Access via Modern Health mobile app.
