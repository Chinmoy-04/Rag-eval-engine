# Synthetic Data Generation, Filtering & Safety Policy
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Soren Lindqvist, Head of Fine-Tuning & Alignment (soren.lindqvist@helixforge.example / CC-2020). Approved by: Devon Hale (CTO) and Elena Voss (CISO). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Scope & Objective
Synthetic data generation plays a vital role in training, aligning, and stress-testing HelixForge foundation models and instruction-following agentic runtimes. This policy governs synthetic dataset curation, automated quality filtering, benchmark decontamination, and ethical review across all research initiatives.

## 2. Benchmark Decontamination & Quality Filtering
To maintain rigorous, unbiased evaluation benchmarks (managed by model_eval_ethics_policy.md):
1. **N-Gram Decontamination**: All generated synthetic data pools must undergo 13-gram substring overlap scanning against standard evaluation test splits (e.g. MMLU, GSM8K, HumanEval, and internal HelixForge Safety Benchmarks).
2. **Overlap Threshold**: Any synthetic sample exhibiting >= 80% lexical similarity to a protected evaluation test set must be automatically dropped from training corpora.
3. **Reward Model Scoring**: Synthetically generated multi-turn conversations must achieve a minimum reward score of **>= 0.72** from the primary HelixForge Alignment Judge model before inclusion in fine-tuning runs.

## 3. Copyright and Licensing Safeguards
- Synthetic datasets generated by prompting external commercial LLM APIs (e.g., OpenAI or Anthropic baselines per software_catalog.csv) must strictly adhere to vendor terms of service.
- Synthetic corpora used to train commercial customer-facing models must be generated exclusively from internal HelixForge base models or open-weight models with commercial Apache 2.0 / MIT licensing (see open_source_contribution_policy.md).

## 4. Safety Guardrails & PII Scrubbing
- **Automated PII Filtration**: Synthetic outputs must pass through automated regex and Named Entity Recognition (NER) scrubbers to ensure no synthetic hallucinations match real personal data (credit card formats, SSNs, real phone numbers).
- **Toxic Pattern Purge**: Synthetic data batches with severe toxicity scores > **0.005** are automatically quarantined for manual review by the Safety & Benchmarks team (cost_centers.csv CC-2030).
