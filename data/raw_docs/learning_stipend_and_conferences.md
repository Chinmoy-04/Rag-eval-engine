# Continuous Learning Stipend & Conference Policy
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Priya Nair, VP Business Operations / People Ops (priya.nair@helixforge.example / CC-4010). Approved by: Mara Chen (CEO). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Annual Learning & Development (L&D) Stipend
HelixForge invests in continuous professional development across our ~420 FTEs and eligible long-term contractors. 

- **Annual Allowance**: **,500 USD** (or €2,300 EUR / S,350 SGD) per employee per fiscal year (February 1 – January 31).
- **Eligible Expenses**:
  - Technical books, e-books, and reference subscriptions (e.g., ACM Digital Library, O'Reilly).
  - Online certifications and courses (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, CISA/CISSP, DeepLearning.AI).
  - University executive education or graduate tuition directly related to the employee's job family (per leveling_rubric.md).
- **Ineligible Expenses**: Home office furniture, general leisure apps, hardware (covered separately under cost_centers.csv CC-4050).

## 2. Conference Attendance & Speaking Budget
HelixForge encourages team members to present research and engage with the broader open-source and AI systems community.

- **Attendee Budget**: Up to **,000 USD** per fiscal year covering registration tickets, economy airfare, and lodging.
- **Accepted Conferences**: NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, OSDI, SOSP, KubeCon, IEEE S&P, USENIX ATC, CVPR.
- **Accepted Speakers / Paper Authors**: Fully covered travel and lodging expenses outside the ,000 attendee cap, funded directly by department cost centers (cost_centers.csv CC-2010 for Applied Research, CC-1010 for Platform Engineering).

## 3. Approval and Expense Reimbursement
1. L&D requests under ,000 can be expensed directly via Expensify with manager pre-approval.
2. Conference travel and L&D programs exceeding ,000 require Department Head approval per approval_matrix.csv.
3. Submissions must include itemized receipts, course completion certificates, or conference badges within **30 days** of expense incurrence.
