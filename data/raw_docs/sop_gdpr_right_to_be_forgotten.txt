1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This SOP establishes the exact technical and operational procedure for executing 
Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) and Right to be Forgotten (RTBF) erasure requests 
under GDPR (EU-West), PDPA (Singapore), and CCPA/CPRA across HelixForge systems.

2. STATUTORY TIMELINES & INTAKE
- Intake Channel: Dedicated portal at privacy.helixforge.example or email to privacy@helixforge.example.
- Legal Verification SLA: Identity verification completed within 5 business days by Legal.
- Statutory Execution Deadline: Total completion within 30 calendar days of verified receipt.

3. STEP-BY-STEP ERASURE WORKFLOW
Step 3.1: Ticket Generation: Legal logs request under PRIV-DSR Jira project with anonymized Subject UUID.
Step 3.2: Primary Database Deletion: Data Platform team triggers erasure script across Postgres metadata clusters:
          $ hf-privacy purge --subject-uuid <UUID> --mode hard-delete
Step 3.3: Vector Embedding Purge: Embeddings associated with customer fine-tuning sets are scrubbed from Pinecone / Milvus vector stores within 48 hours.
Step 3.4: Cache Eviction: Purge Redis and edge inference caches (edge-global) per data_residency_matrix.csv.
Step 3.5: Training Data Isolation: If customer data was processed for fine-tuning, verify dataset quarantine logs in Applied Research (CC-2020).
Step 3.6: Backup Scrubber Register: Deletion tombstone added to backup compaction daemon. Cold archive backups will overwrite tombstones within 90 days per records_retention.md.

4. LEGAL HOLD & EXCEPTIONS
If the subject is under an active litigation hold or tax audit hold (per legal_hold.md), the erasure is suspended. Legal issues a formal exemption notice to the requester within 10 business days.

5. AUDIT CERTIFICATION
Upon completion, the Privacy Lead generates a cryptographically signed Certificate of Destruction (SHA-256 hash) archived in compliance vault for SOC 2 Type II audit inspection.

6. RELATED POLICIES & CROSS-LINKS
- Data residency and cloud partitions: data_residency_matrix.csv.
- Records retention and backup lifecycle: records_retention.md and disaster_recovery_rpo_rto.md.
- Legal department cost center: cost_centers.csv (CC-4030).
