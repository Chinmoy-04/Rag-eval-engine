# Equity Grants, Refresh Cadence, and Options Policy
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Julian Sterling, Finance Controller (julian.sterling@helixforge.example / CC-4020). Approved by: Mara Chen (CEO) and Devon Hale (CTO). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Plan Overview & Governance
HelixForge provides equity compensation to align long-term employee incentives with company growth and enterprise valuation. All equity grants consist of Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) for US employees or Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs) / Enterprise Management Incentives (EMI) for international employees in Dublin and Singapore.

## 2. Initial Grants and Standard Vesting Schedule
- **Initial Grant Sizing**: Determined at hire based on the candidate's role family and level per compensation_bands.csv.
- **Vesting Schedule**: Standard **4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff** (25% vests at Month 12, followed by equal quarterly vesting over the remaining 36 months).
- **Vesting Commencement Date**: Aligned with the employee's official start date or the first day of the fiscal quarter following Board approval.

## 3. Annual Equity Refresh Program
- **Eligibility**: Full-time employees who have completed at least **12 months of continuous tenure** and achieve a performance rating of " Meets Expectations\ or higher during the August/February review cycle.
- **Refresh Sizing**: Calibrated to 25%–50% of the annual equity target for the employee's current level (per compensation_bands.csv).
- **Refresh Vesting**: 4-year vesting with **no cliff**; vests in equal quarterly installments over 16 quarters beginning immediately upon grant date.

## 4. Exercise Windows and Secondary Liquidity
- **Post-Termination Exercise Period (PTEP)**: Employees with >= 2 years tenure receive an extended **5-year exercise window** for vested options upon voluntary departure (extended from standard 90 days).
- **Secondary Share Sales**: Annual company-sponsored secondary tender offers are subject to Board approval and restricted to vested shares held by employees with >= 18 months tenure.
