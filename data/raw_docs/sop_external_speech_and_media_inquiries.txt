1. PURPOSE & CORE PRINCIPLE
This SOP governs interactions with journalists, conference presentations, podcast 
appearances, and external research publications for all ~420 FTEs and contractors. 
Only designated corporate spokespersons are authorized to speak on behalf of HelixForge.

2. AUTHORIZED CORPORATE SPOKESPERSONS
- Company Strategy, Financials & Vision: Mara Chen (CEO).
- Technical Architecture & AI Research: Devon Hale (CTO) or designated research authors.
- Security, Privacy & Vulnerabilities: Elena Voss (CISO) or Clara Dupont (Legal Lead).
- People Ops & Workplace: Priya Nair (VP Business Operations).

3. INTAKE WORKFLOW FOR PRESS & MEDIA
Step 3.1: Inbound Media Inquiries: Forward all inbound press requests immediately to press@helixforge.example. Do not provide on-the-record or off-the-record comments without PR clearance.
Step 3.2: PR Triage SLA: Communications team responds to reporter inquiries within 4 business hours.
Step 3.3: Briefing Prep: Comms coordinates briefing prep sessions and approved talking points with the designated executive.

4. CONFERENCE SPEAKING & RESEARCH PAPER PRE-CLEARANCE
- Paper Submissions: Research manuscripts destined for arXiv, NeurIPS, ICML, or CVPR must be submitted to the Research Review Board (cost_centers.csv CC-2010) at least 10 business days prior to the conference submission deadline.
- Intellectual Property Screen: Legal verifies that manuscripts do not disclose unpatented proprietary model architectures or trade secrets.
- Conference Travel Funding: Approved speakers receive fully covered travel per learning_stipend_and_conferences.md.
