1. PURPOSE & CORE PRINCIPLE
This SOP governs communication hygiene, channel structures, and retention policies 
across HelixForge Slack Enterprise Grid. 

CORE CANON RULE: The written Knowledge Base (wiki/docs) is the single source of truth 
for company policy, engineering architecture, and operational procedures. Slack 
conversations, DMs, and thread consensus DO NOT constitute formal policy or binding approvals.

2. GLOBAL COLLABORATION WINDOW & ASYNC NORMS
- Core Collaboration Window: **14:00–17:00 UTC** across Austin HQ, Dublin, and Singapore.
- Outside core hours, team members are expected to work asynchronously. Immediate responses outside local office hours (09:30–17:30 local) are NOT expected or required unless an individual is on active on-call duty (per oncall_escalation_matrix.csv).
- Do Not Disturb (DND) modes must be respected across all international time zones.

3. CHANNEL TAXONOMY & NAMING CONVENTIONS
All corporate public and private channels must adhere to standardized prefixes:
- #proj-<name>: Cross-functional initiative channels with active milestones.
- #team-<name>: Departmental workstreams (e.g. #team-serving, #team-secops).
- #help-<name>: Public internal support triage (e.g. #help-it, #help-peopleops, #help-procurement).
- #announce-<name>: One-way executive broadcasts (e.g. #announce-global, #announce-eng).
- #inc-<id>: Dedicated war room channels for SEV-1/SEV-2 active incidents (auto-generated by PagerDuty).

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION & SECURITY RESTRICTIONS
- Restricted Data Prohibition: API keys, database credentials, production private keys, customer PII, and raw model weights must NEVER be pasted into Slack.
- Ephemeral Retention: Slack message history is subject to a mandatory **90-day automatic retention purge** per records_retention.md. Important decisions must be transcribed to Notion/GitHub ADRs.
