1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This SOP defines the rapid-response war room mechanics, cross-functional bridge 
coordination, and customer communications during SEV-1 critical platform outages 
affecting Enterprise Tier 1 and Tier 2 customers.

2. WAR ROOM ACTIVATION SEQUENCE
Step 2.1: Incident Trigger: Customer Support Engineer or Datadog Synthetic Monitor detects API degradation exceeding SEV-1 criteria (> 2% API error rate).
Step 2.2: PagerDuty Bridge: Automated PagerDuty escalation pages Cloud Infra on-call primary and Rachel Adams within 15 minutes (per customer_support_slas.md).
Step 2.3: Slack Incident Room: PagerDuty auto-provisions dedicated channel #inc-<incident_id> and Zoom war room bridge.
Step 2.4: Role Assignment:
          - Incident Commander (IC): Leads technical remediation (Chloe Bennett or Senior SRE).
          - Comms Lead: Manages status page and executive updates (Rachel Adams).
          - Scribe: Records timeline actions in incident log.

3. CUSTOMER BRIDGE & STATUS PAGE CADENCE
- 25-Minute Bridge SLA: For Enterprise Tier 1 customers, the Comms Lead opens a dedicated external customer Zoom bridge within 25 minutes of ticket receipt.
- Status Page Updates: Public status post (status.helixforge.example) updated every 30 minutes per status_page_rules.md.
- External statements must be sanitized to exclude internal server hostnames, customer names, or proprietary model weight paths.

4. INCIDENT DOWNGRADE & POST-MORTEM HANDOFF
Step 4.1: Service Restoration: Error rates return to baseline (<0.01%) for 60 continuous minutes.
Step 4.2: Downgrade to Monitoring: Status page updated to 'Monitoring'.
Step 4.3: Post-Mortem Ticket: IC creates RCA-<incident_id> Jira ticket. 72-hour wiki draft SLA begins immediately (see security_incident_postmortem_guidelines.md).

5. RELATED POLICIES & CROSS-LINKS
