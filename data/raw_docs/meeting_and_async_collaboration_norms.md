# Meeting Cadence & Asynchronous Collaboration Norms
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Priya Nair, VP Business Operations / People Ops (priya.nair@helixforge.example / CC-4010). Approved by: Mara Chen (CEO) and Devon Hale (CTO). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Operating Philosophy
HelixForge operates as a distributed, high-autonomy engineering and research organization across Austin, Dublin, and Singapore. To preserve deep focus time for complex systems programming and model training, asynchronous written communication is our default operating mode. The written KB is the source of truth; Slack threads and transient verbal chats do not constitute company policy.

## 2. Core Collaboration Window & Time Zone Etiquette
- **Global Core Hours**: **14:00–17:00 UTC** (09:00–12:00 Austin CST, 14:00–17:00 Dublin GMT/IST, 22:00–01:00 Singapore SGT).
- All cross-regional meetings, Architectural Review Boards (ARBs), and company-wide broadcasts must occur strictly within this 3-hour window.
- Respect local office hours (09:30–17:30 local time). Team members in Singapore or Dublin are never required to attend synchronous meetings scheduled outside their normal working hours unless on active on-call rotation (per oncall_escalation_matrix.csv).

## 3. Meeting Governance & Structure Rules
1. **No-Meeting Wednesdays**: Every Wednesday is designated company-wide as an uninterrupted Focus Day. No internal recurring or ad-hoc meetings may be scheduled.
2. **Speedy Meetings**: Default meeting durations are **25 minutes** (for 30-min slots) and **50 minutes** (for 60-min slots) to provide 5–10 minute transition buffers.
3. **Mandatory Written Agenda**: Every calendar invite must include a written agenda and target decision outcome. If no agenda is provided at least **2 hours prior**, invitees are empowered to decline.
4. **Pre-Read Culture**: For meetings exceeding 3 participants, materials (e.g. PRDs, ADRs) must be distributed 24 hours in advance. The first 10 minutes of the meeting are dedicated to silent reading.

## 4. Async Communication Turnaround SLAs
- **Slack Mentions / DMs**: Expected response within **4 business hours** during local working hours.
- **GitHub Pull Request Reviews**: L4+ engineers must review PRs within **24 business hours** (see leveling_rubric.md).
- **Email Inquiries**: Response expected within **1 business day**.
