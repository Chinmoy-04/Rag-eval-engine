# Facilities, Badge Access, and Visitor Security Policy
*HelixForge fictional handbook.*

Owner: Brendan Walsh, Head of Facilities & Global Real Estate (brendan.walsh@helixforge.example / CC-4040). Approved by: Elena Voss (CISO) and Priya Nair (VP Business Operations). Effective date: February 1, 2026.

## 1. Physical Sites & Standard Office Hours
HelixForge operates three corporate facilities:
- **Austin HQ**: 500 West 2nd St, Suite 1900, Austin, TX 78701 (Capacity: 320 desks, Server Lab, Executive Suite).
- **Dublin Office**: 1 Windmill Lane, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin 2, Ireland (Capacity: 80 desks).
- **Singapore Office**: 8 Marina Boulevard, Marina Bay Financial Centre Tower 1, Singapore 018981 (Capacity: 60 desks).

Standard office hours across all sites are **09:30–17:30 local time**. The global core collaboration window is **14:00–17:00 UTC**.

## 2. Badge Issuance & Physical Access Controls
1. **Employee & Contractor Badges**: All ~420 FTEs and ~60 contractors are issued RFID HID Seos smart credentials on Day 1 by Nathan Brooks (Workplace IT / CC-4050).
2. **Access Zones**:
   - *Zone 1 (General Office & Commons)*: Accessible 24/7 to all badge holders.
   - *Zone 2 (Executive Boardrooms & People Ops Archive)*: Restricted to L5+ Managers and HR personnel.
   - *Zone 3 (Server Lab & Hardware Engineering)*: Multi-factor physical biometric + YubiKey tap required. Restricted to Platform Engineering hardware leads (Marcus Vance / CC-1010).
3. **Lost or Stolen Badges**: Must be reported to facilities-security@helixforge.example within **2 hours**. Deactivation in the Brivo access control system is executed immediately.

## 3. Visitor Registration and Escort Policy
- All external visitors (vendors, interview candidates, investors) must pre-register via Envoy at least **24 hours** prior to arrival.
- Visitors must sign the HelixForge Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) at the reception kiosk and display a temporary red visitor badge at all times.
- Visitors must remain escorted by their designated HelixForge host at all times within Zone 1 and are strictly barred from Zone 3.

## 4. Parking and Commuter Benefits
- **Austin HQ**: Dedicated badge-access garage on San Antonio St. Electric vehicle charging stations available on Level B2 (complimentary 4 hours/day).
- **Dublin & Singapore**: Public transit subsidy administered via People Ops ( USD equivalent / month).
