1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This SOP defines the technical and operational steps for executing departures for 
full-time employees (~420 FTEs) and contractors (~60 personnel) across Austin, 
Dublin, Singapore, and remote locations.

2. OFFBOARDING INITIATION & NOTICE TIMELINES
- Voluntary Resignation: Employee provides written notice to People Ops. Standard notice period is 2 weeks (US) or 1 month (Dublin / Singapore).
- Involuntary Termination: People Ops coordinates with Legal (Clara Dupont) and CISO (Elena Voss) to synchronize access revocation.

3. ACCESS REVOCATION SEQUENCE
Step 3.1: Scheduled Suspension: Exactly at **17:30 local time** on departure date, Okta SSO triggers automated de-provisioning webhooks:
          - Suspends Google Workspace, Slack Enterprise Grid, Notion, and Jira.
          - Revokes HashiCorp Vault tokens and active AWS/GCP IAM sessions.
Step 3.2: YubiKey & Certificate Invalidation: Workplace IT revokes SSH certificates and removes YubiKey serial numbers from the corporate bastion whitelist.
Step 3.3: 1Password Vault Cleanup: Shared team vaults and private employee vaults are purged and deleted.
Step 3.4: MDM Remote Lock: Jamf Pro / Intune issues remote lock command to laptop and selective wipe to enrolled mobile devices per byod_and_mobile_device_policy.md.

4. PHYSICAL HARDWARE RECOVERY & REIMBURSEMENT
- Departing employee must return company-owned laptops and monitors within **14 calendar days**.
- Prepaid insured FedEx/DHL return kit dispatched to employee home address on Day 1 post-departure.
- If hardware is not returned within 14 days, remote cryptographic wipe is executed per sop_laptop_imaging_deprovisioning.txt.

5. FINAL PAYROLL, EQUITY & BENEFITS
- Unused accrued PTO (up to the 8-day rollover cap) is paid out in final paycheck per state/country labor laws.
- Vested stock options enter the 5-year Post-Termination Exercise Period (PTEP) for employees with >= 2 years tenure (see equity_refresh_and_options_policy.md).
